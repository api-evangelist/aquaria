# Aquaria

> Aquaria builds atmospheric water generators (AWGs) that source pure, filtered water directly from the air. Our Hydropack systems capture humidity, run it through multi-stage filtration, and deliver reliable water to homes and sites that can't depend on municipal supply, wells, or rainfall. Built for homeowners, developers, and communities working toward water resilience.

Aquaria's mission is to make water resilience as normal as energy resilience. Every drop passes through multi-stage filtration and UV treatment. In independent third-party lab testing across 100+ substances, Aquaria water came back non-detect or below EPA maximum contaminant levels in the samples tested — no microplastics, PFAS, or dissolved heavy metals detected. Water is independently certified by Amlabs Intertek (international) and SimpleLab (US). The brand leads with verification, real measurements, and real customer stories rather than hype. Aquaria pairs naturally with solar installations for a self-sustaining, off-grid water source.

Aquaria is an American company founded in 2021 by brothers Brian Sheng (co-founder & CEO) and Eric Sheng (co-founder & COO). It was named one of TIME's Best Inventions of 2024, and its founders were named to the Forbes 30 Under 30 list.
